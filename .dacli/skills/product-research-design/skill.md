# product-research-design
Separate observed evidence, inference, and hypothesis. Research recent concrete behavior rather than hypothetical preference; require corroboration before calling a need confirmed. Tie proposals to existing substrate, cost, risk, and a falsifiable success signal. Specify data, empty/error/loading states, accessibility, responsive behavior, and non-goals so an implementer and reviewer can independently agree on done.
